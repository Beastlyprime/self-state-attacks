# Azamat Satybaldy

_Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azamat_Satybaldy_  
_Revision: 1334843264_

Azamat Satybaldy (Kazakh: Азамат Сатыбалды; born 2 November 1977) is a Kazakhstani actor of drama theater, cinema, producer, and television presenter. He is an Honored Artist of Kazakhstan (2014). He is also the Dean of Zhurgenov Kazakh National Academy of Arts (since 14 February 2022).
